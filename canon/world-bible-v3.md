*(All v2 content stands. One item resolved:)*

- **RULING (locked): Brutus's arrival vs. the peace cracking.** Robert's answer:
  Brutus **disrupts the status quo and accelerates already-brewing hostile
  events.** He is not the sole cause of the wars — they were already forming —

  his effect on the world, not a specific plot beat to hit.

### 4.6 — The peoples of the seven nations: LOCKED (physical features and temperament)

**RULING (locked, Robert directly, 2026-10-01): the seven peoples are as written
below.** Recorded exactly as he wrote them, typos included. Tier A going forward.
Read with §4.new: the setting is an island, so every people below is an island
people.

**Marium**
> Roman people if a bit more brawny and bronze from life on an island.
> Even among his peers, brutus is considered exceptionally vigorous

**Themelios**
> Greece-ish. A lot more diverse due to being run by merchants. Do not let their pleasant demeanor fool you. They are as likely to gossip about you negatively as inviting you for dinner.

**Reyjar**
> Porcelain, cold skin. White-blonde-grey-Black hair . High cheekbones, slight natural sunkeness in their eyes. Slender. Relatively tall.

**Li Trice**
> Vary fron petite to average height. Voluptuous lower half for the women. The men have more of a stockier body, while able to still be agile. Almond hair to black. Red brown black eyes. Beauty marks on face, arms legs. Forefront of fashion that emphasizes dramatic colors and body contour.

**Ayusti**
> Light tan, energetic, extroverted, cute, petite to average height. Face leands to be more Japanese esque.

**Beloufi**
> These people are very physically well endowed. 6-7 feet. Their desire to show love to the people they care about is matched by their appetite for making merry. The most loyal people you will ever meet. Should you bring disgrace and humiliation to one, expect a quick and severe retribution. More tears will fall from the beloufi you betrayed than from you missing half your teeth and swollen black eyes. And those are the gentle ones. The others tend to take no nonsense bullshit and settle disputes quickly. They are strong enough to conquer the entire world. They just don't care for the misery it will cause.

**Edinius**
> Direct. Curt. Pale white. Light green. Their colors. conservative wear, dignified. Muted colors. Dry humor. Tall. Lithuanian face shape and eyes.

**Compiler's readings (NOT canon; flagged so Robert can correct them):**
- **Marium:** "his peers" is read as Brutus's fellow Mariumites. The entry sits under
  Marium but does not itself state Brutus's nation. It records physical vigour only:
  **Brutus's core trait (brave/reckless vs. lazy) stays reserved for Robert (§3).**
- **Reyjar:** "sunkeness" is read as "sunkenness": a slight natural hollowing around
  the eyes (deep-set eyes).
- **Li Trice:** "Almond hair" is read as the warm light brown of an almond, running
  to black; "Red brown black eyes" as eye colours from red-brown to brown to black.
- **Ayusti:** the face leans Japanese; skin (light tan) and temperament are as stated.
- **Beloufi:** "very physically well endowed" is read as physically large and
  powerfully built, which fits "6-7 feet". "They just don't care for the misery it will
  cause" is read as *dislike*: they could conquer the world and don't, because they
  don't like the misery it would cause. (If "don't care for" meant *indifferent to*,
  that reverses the meaning. Please confirm.)
- **Edinius:** "Pale white. Light green. Their colors." is read as the national
  colours (pale white and light green) with pale skin. If "light green" also means
  eye colour, please say.

**Proposed continuation (zero canon; written by the compiler to carry the rulings
forward, in the same register, and to be reviewed per entry):**

- **Marium.** The island has made the Roman a heavier, harder thing: broad backs and
  thick forearms from hauling nets, cutting stone and rowing, skin bronzed by a life
  on water and rock, with the old Roman gravitas worn over a body built for work.
  Strength is ordinary here, so being *exceptionally* vigorous even among them is a
  mark of distinction. Proposed guild fit: Lantern Company.
- **Themelios.** A republic of counting-houses more than temples, run by merchants, so
  travellers of every origin have settled here and the crowd looks like no single
  people. Hospitality is a trade skill: a dinner invitation is an investment, a
  rumour is a loan, and both are offered with the same warm smile. Reputation is the
  currency, so everyone keeps a quiet ledger of favours and slights, and the host who
  just poured your wine may already have described you at three other tables.
  Proposed guild fit: The Hollow Crown.
- **Reyjar.** Pale as porcelain and cool to the touch, with hair from white-blonde
  through ash-grey to a startling black, high cheekbones and slightly hollow eyes that
  make every face look watchful. Slender and relatively tall; people who never look
  quite warm. (Robert specified looks only. Temperament here is proposed: formal,
  restrained, slow to warm, ceremony over spontaneity, with black lace and iron and a
  crimson lining as the only heat.) Proposed guild fit: The Iron Oath.
- **Li Trice.** Petite to average, built to be looked at and dressed to be: women full
  through the hip and thigh, men stockier but quick on their feet; almond-brown to
  black hair; red-brown to black eyes; beauty marks scattered over face, arms and
  legs, treated as ornament and sometimes added with paint. They are the island's
  forefront of fashion: clothing cut close to the body in dramatic colour, so a Li
  Trice crowd looks like a painting. Everyone else copies them a season late. Proposed
  guild fit: Gilt Hand.
- **Ayusti.** Light tan, bright and quick: energetic, extroverted and cute, petite to
  average, with faces that lean Japanese. They talk with their hands, greet strangers
  like old friends, and treat every task as a reason to make noise. Proposed guild fit:
  The Brethren of Saint Piran, whose enthusiasm is the national temperament turned
  into a vocation.
- **Beloufi.** Giants of six to seven feet, powerfully built, who love with the same
  force they feast, drink and sing, and are the most loyal people on the island:
  friends are family. Disgrace or humiliation done to one of them brings quick,
  severe retribution; the betrayed Beloufi weeps harder than the traitor does after
  the beating, and those are the gentle ones. The rest take no nonsense and settle
  disputes fast and bluntly. Collectively they could conquer everything and do not,
  because they dislike the misery it would cause. Proposed guild fit: The Merry Rabble.
- **Edinius.** Direct and curt, pale-skinned and tall, with Lithuanian face shapes and
  eyes. Their colours are pale white and light green; dress is conservative,
  dignified and muted; humour is dry. A thing is said once, plainly, and silence is
  not rudeness. Proposed guild fit: The Black Candle.
