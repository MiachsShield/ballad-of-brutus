## 2. The World's Nature (Tier A, with one addition)

*(All v2 content stands. One item resolved:)*

- **RULING (locked): Brutus's arrival vs. the peace cracking.** Robert's answer:
  Brutus **disrupts the status quo and accelerates already-brewing hostile
  events.** He is not the sole cause of the wars — they were already forming —
  but his presence speeds up what was coming. This is **narrative-writing
  guidance**, not a scene-level causal mechanism — it tells writers how to frame
  his effect on the world, not a specific plot beat to hit.

### 4.new — Geography: LOCKED

**RULING (locked): the setting is an ISLAND.** Sealed off — no further geographic
detail (size, neighboring landmasses, sea routes beyond what's already stated) is
to be developed until Robert revisits this. Treat as settled but minimal; do not
extrapolate continent-scale logistics, additional landmasses, or archipelago
structure from this ruling.

### 4.5 — Faction/political-layer status: PARTIALLY RESOLVED

**RULING (locked): the political/national layer is a separate system from guild
economic activity — not the same thing described two ways, and not a 1:1 mapping
of the seven nations onto factions.** This closes the v1 open question of whether
the guild economy (Fame/Reputation/Marketability, GvG hostility actions, crabs-in-
bucket, etc.) and the worldmap political layer are the same system — they are
confirmed **distinct**. What remains open: the exact cardinality and structure of
nation-to-faction mapping itself (how many factions per nation, whether factions
cross national lines) — not resolved by this ruling, only the "is it the same as
guild activity" question is closed.

## 6. Bestiary & Dungeon System — RESOLVED

**RULING (locked): the real-time dungeon combat system (flow/punish, HP/energy
degradation axes, Skulker/Brute/Stalker archetypes — the system actually built,
playtested, and iterated on across six rounds) is canonical going forward and
supersedes Doc 13's earlier dice-roll-movement, lightweight-resolver dungeon
design.** Doc 13 is deprecated for dungeon mechanics. The Doc 21 proposed
100-monster bestiary was written against Doc 13's system — it should be treated as
a flavor/concept reference only until reviewed against the real-time system's
actual needs, not as ready-to-port content.

This also closes the file-lineage question from the ownership-manifest discussion:
the real-time dungeon prototype is the canonical dungeon-combat line going
forward, not a competing branch.
