# Robert's decisions — 2026-09-18

## 1. Settlement vs tribute — BOTH
**Asked:** Are settlement and tribute two separate player-facing systems, or
one system doing both jobs?
**Robert:** "Both."
Settlement (escalating concessions until hostility is spent) and tribute (an
action spent every turn with a success/failure/refusal ladder) are two
separate systems. Design docs should treat them as distinct player choices.

## 2. Region names are the canon nations — YES
**Asked:** Are the seven UI region names the seven canon nations?
**Robert:** "yes"
The seven named regions in the UI are the seven canon nations. The region map
(C1) is a display layer over guild-ownership data using these names; no
separate nation list.

## 3. Signature card access — refined
**Asked:** Claude's cross-class-signature question: who can use Signature cards?
**Robert:** "Signature cards can be used as long as they retain what gives the
Signature access. Unique units, abilities, and magic gear can give access to
abilities despite what class they are."
Refines the 2026-09-17 R5 three-tier ruling (General / Class / Signature):
Signature access is granted by a unique character, an ability, or a piece of
magic gear — and the card stays usable as long as the holder retains that
source of access, regardless of class.
