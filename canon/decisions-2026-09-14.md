# Robert's settled design decisions — 2026-09-14

Binding on all lanes. These override any brief. Answered on the 12-question
gating sheet; recorded here so no model re-litigates them.

1. **Companion 0 HP:** banked for playtest. Open hypothesis — with an
   abundance of adventurers, will players still feel loss aversion even with
   stronger allies on the horizon?

2. **Expedition XP:** yes — surviving deployed adventurers earn XP after
   every defeated enemy.

3. **Ambush and formal-war deaths:** count as murder. Robert: "Not fair, but
   what is."

4. **Crime:** anything that causes the loss of an acquired asset or distress
   to an active member of the guild.

5. **Brutus's noncombat death:** Brutus may die in events — to be elaborated
   during narrative and guild-war writing. No generic Brutus deaths for now.

6. **Takeover conclusion:** an affection response — welcome Brutus as new
   boss / reluctant / indifferent / desert / refuse.

7. **Settlement:** concessions and labor escalate until hostility is
   exhausted; reducing low hostility becomes progressively more expensive.
   Exceptional outcomes may earn affiliate treatment instead of "whipped dog"
   treatment.

8. **Tribute/appeasement:** spend action(s) every turn; urgent missions may
   override other urgent work. Success earns appreciation, failure earns
   punishment, refusal earns severe punishment and possibly multiple
   onslaughts.

9. **Win progress:** every region visibly under Brutus is sufficient.

10. **Energy costs:** do not rescale unless scaling is itself an effect.
    Card costs remain on Brutus's 20-point bar.

11. **Second Wind:** a card that displays only while stunned — never occupies
    a useful slot while irrelevant. Overrides Claude's cut recommendation
    and Astra's exclusion.

12. **Player-held territory:** guilds dying outside Brutus's ownership leave
    replaceable slots; when Brutus owns territory, he permits no other
    leaders.
