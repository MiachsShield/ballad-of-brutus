# Robert's decisions: card faces and card readability (2026-09-17)

Relayed by Claude word for word. Binding per repo precedence: `canon/` >
build > design docs.

## 1. Who is on the card face
**Asked:** "Should Priest/Warrior card art show Brutus himself, or a class
figure (the current hooded priest)?"
**Robert:** "Latter. Grok already privy"

Card faces for class cards show a class figure, not Brutus.

## 2. Category readability
**Asked:** "Fold the 22 old flagged faces into the re-colour pass instead
of Kavi uploading the old list?"
**Robert (first pass):** "Honestly stumped. Ofc players want to easily
tell the card. But garish colors make it look ugly."

**Robert (ruling):** "Top symbol on card overlay. Red sword , blue shield
etc. Behind name of card, over art"

A coloured category symbol sits at the top of the card overlay, behind the
card name, over the art. Spec in `docs/card-guidelines/readability.md`.

## 3. Rarity
**Asked:** "Switch rarity colours (green/blue/red) to something like
bronze/silver/gold/violet?"
**Robert:** "Rarity should be apparent but not by make; there can be
extremely valuable bronze gear and such. Use embedded gems or something"

Rarity is carried by embedded gems, not by the frame's material. Frame
metal is flavour only.

## 4. Earlier answer, carried here for completeness
The 22 older flagged faces are folded into Grok's G4 re-light pass; there
is no separate brief.
