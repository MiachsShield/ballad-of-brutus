## 5. Who can use which cards
**Asked:** "Cross-class signatures: (A) shared pool or (B) guaranteed
own-class."
**Robert:** "Cards are either general- allowed in all, class restriction-
warrior only, signature- restricted to unique characters, abilities, or
gear"

This replaces the A/B framing. There are three access tiers:
- **General:** allowed in every deck.
- **Class:** restricted to one class (e.g. Warrior only).
- **Signature:** restricted to a unique character, an ability, or a piece
  of gear.
