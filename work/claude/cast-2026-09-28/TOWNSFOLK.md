# Townsfolk: the lovable, petty, shallow crowd

**Status: Tier B draft (zero canon).** Drafted 2026-10-03 from Robert's quick answers
(not yet in the world bible; he said to keep asking before writing it up). Nothing here
is built into the sim. It maps the seven sim personality traits to town types, since
Robert said staple types come from "personality types and how they affect the person".

## The feel (Robert's rules, paraphrased)

- A crowd of comic, lovable jerks, like the townsfolk of South Park, Bikini Bottom and
  Springfield. People are not their political identity: crowns and councils are
  background noise they grumble about. The nation only tints how a trait comes out.
- The comedy lives in **petty squabbles, gossip and reputation, and absurd
  overreactions**, with restraint. **Nothing immersion-breaking. No toilet humour and no
  perverted humour, for the most part.** Mean but harmless, and sometimes genuinely dark.
- **The town is shallow and unkind, and that is the joke, never the narrator's verdict.**
  Those with no looks, no talent and no power (strength, money, connections or magic) are
  not needed, so they tend to be unemployed. They gather in the **slums**, which the town
  treats with **disdain**. They still have desires and keep striving, and they bond through
  shared exclusion. Some certainly try to catch a break.
- When an adventurer dies, **the town only cares if they were beautiful and young**,
  not for some sod nobody knows. Rumours between capitals travel **fast and garbled**.
- **The recurring faces are the generics who survive.** They have goals, grievances,
  squabbles and crushes: people trying to eke out a living.
- Every region has a labor-market town, and it is that region's capital. Roaming between
  them is limited by fame or money. (The playtest slice stays a single town.)

## Every townsperson has three goals and one core want

- **Short goal** (this week), **mid goal** (this season), **long goal** (a life ambition).
- **The core want** is the human desire that drives their actions. It is always one of:
  **respect, belonging, security, love, revenge, escape**, never an object or a gag. Gags
  decorate the short goals only. (Robert: the core desire cannot be a burger.)

## Trait to townsperson

Each person carries two traits, as the adventurers do: the first sets the type, the second
colours it (a proud, greedy person is a stuck-up skinflint).

| Trait | Town type | The daily flaw | Squabble and gossip | Usual core want | In the slums |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| proud | The Braggart | tells everyone how it should be done | takes every correction as a duel | respect | insists he is "between opportunities" |
| greedy | The Skinflint | haggles over everything, counts your coin | keeps a running tally of who owes what | security | runs a tiny, ruthless lending ring of buttons |
| vengeful | The Grudge-Keeper | files every slight in a mental ledger | a thirty-year feud over a borrowed ladle | revenge | the neighbourhood's unofficial historian of wrongs |
| cautious | The Doom Crank | sees a plot behind every delay | spreads garbled warnings before the facts arrive | security | convinced the empty jobs board is a conspiracy |
| kind | The Busybody | helps whether or not asked, and cannot say no | organises other people's lives and sulks when ignored | belonging | feeds everyone and is quietly the glue |
| reckless | The Daredevil Fool | bets on himself against all evidence | dares, dares back, and then tells it wrong | respect or escape | volunteers for every terrible odd job |
| pragmatic | The Cynic | prices everything, trusts nothing | dry, accurate and quietly smug | security or escape | the sharpest mind in the slums, with no door to knock on |

## How the nation tints the type

Each nation's established flaw colours how a trait comes out (from the locked rulings in
the world bible, section 4.7). The type stays the same.

| Nation | The tint |
|---|---|
| Marium | slow to anger, then explosive: a Grudge-Keeper who is calm until he is not |
| Themelios | gossip is currency: every type trades in reputation and rumour |
| Reyjar | praise for feats, scorn for weaklings: a Braggart is celebrated until he fails |
| Li Trice | haughty, shrewd and teasing: a Skinflint with a razor tongue |
| Ayusti | naive, flighty and nosy: a Busybody who wanders off mid-favour |
| Beloufi | loyal one by one, vicious to liars: grudges are blood-deep |
| Edinius | judgmental and joyless: a Doom Crank who is right and unbearable |

## Hooks if Robert approves (proposals only, nothing is built)

1. Give every unnamed newcomer the three goals and a core want, drawn by trait.
2. After a newcomer survives some number of visits, mark them a **regular**: keep their
   name, goals, grudges and crush, and let them recur in the bulletin.
3. Weight the bulletin's grief for a death by **beauty and youth** (Fame and Marketability),
   so a sod's death is a line and a beautiful young one is a lament.
