# Redo brief — Bear Hug Break

- **Name:** Bear Hug Break
- **Class:** Warrior
- **Energy cost / rules text:** Not canon; card doesn't exist in the
  build at all. Only citable numbers are my own audit-design row (design,
  not canon): heavy tier, cost 9, "Dmg 19, breaks free of a grapple/hold
  effect."

## Readability failure (original face, `art/audit/faces/bear-hug-break.jpg`)

"Literal bear; term means breaking a grapple." The original shows an
actual brown bear crushing a boulder, captioned "GRAB-AND-SMASH, COSTLY
HEAVY IMPACT." It takes "bear hug" literally instead of as the idiom (a
grappling hold), and shows a boulder being crushed rather than a
character breaking free of being held.

## Where Grok's first redo still falls short (this is the live blocker on PR #3)

Viewed directly (`work/grok/critical-redos/faces/bear-hug-break.jpg`): the
bear is gone — that part is fixed. But the image shows two men grappling,
one bloodied figure apparently pulling from the other's hold. The two
bodies are staged too similarly and too entangled: the first thing the
eye reads is "two people locked together," not "one person escaping."
This matches Kavi's and Grok's own verdict exactly: **"first read is the
hug, not the break."** I agree — does not pass.

## Art direction for the next attempt

- **Action:** the frame has to show the *moment of breaking*, not the
  hold itself. Stage it as the Warrior's arm/shoulder driving out and
  away from a grappling enemy's arms, with the enemy's grip visibly
  failing — fingers slipping, an arm forced open, torque in the enemy's
  limb. The enemy should be visibly *losing* the hold on-panel, not just
  embracing.
- **Compositional fix:** per "Body in motion — the silhouette should
  carry the action before detail does," the two figures need an
  asymmetric silhouette. One figure's body language should read as
  explosively expanding outward (elbow driving out, chest opening, a
  clean diagonal line away from the grapple); the other's should read as
  being shoved back / losing grip. If both figures read as similarly
  weighted and passive, the eye can't tell who's acting — which is
  exactly the current failure.
- **Crop:** per "C · Ability cards," crop tighter on the point of
  separation itself — the breaking grip, the driving arm/shoulder —
  rather than a wide two-body composition. A tighter crop on the exact
  break point removes the ambiguity Kavi flagged.
- **Cost read:** heavy tier, cost 9 — near-signature weight. The impact
  should read as forceful and costly: a hard, explosive break, not a
  struggle-in-progress. Motion blur or impact lines on the breaking arm
  would help sell "costly heavy impact" (the original caption had the
  right instinct, minus the bear).
- **No bear** — Grok's redo already correctly dropped this; keep it
  dropped.

## Visual-guide citations

- "Body in motion: the silhouette should carry the action before detail
  does" — the single most relevant line for this fix.
- REF 02 (FFXIV — impact & foreshortening): "hands as the first read;
  diagonal force through shoulders and hips." This is the clearest
  reference model for a foreshortened impact that reads instantly, which
  is precisely what this face is missing.
- Action test: "name the move in under a second: strike, guard, lunge,
  **break**, recover" — "break" is one of the five actions the guide's
  own test explicitly expects a viewer to name. This face currently
  fails that named criterion directly.
