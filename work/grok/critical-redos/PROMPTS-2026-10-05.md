## Blinding Halo — Priest, design cost 5 (not canon)

Stylized dark-fantasy ability-card illustration, painterly, Bravely charm. A young Priest in bone-cloth vestments and a short soot-violet stole thrusts one hand forward. A tight directed burst of warm brass light hits an armored enemy's eyes; the enemy flinches, eyes squeezed shut, head snapped back. A second smaller silhouette at the edge of the burst also recoils. Medium burst, not a world-ending flare. Crop on the channeling hand and the target's face. Empty dark dungeon air in the top quarter. No text.

## Stigmata — Priest, design cost 3 (not canon)

Stylized ability card. Young Priest in clerical vestments, cropped to torso and hands. Glowing oxblood wound-marks on both palms, modest warm bone-cloth heal light pouring outward from the open hand. The figure is still advancing, not collapsed. Wound and heal read in the same frame. Restraint, not gore. Empty space in the top quarter. No text.

## Called Shot — Ranger, design cost 3 (not canon)

Stylized ability card. A Ranger in a short weathered coat draws a compact bow. The arrow is already finding a narrow gap at an enemy's knee armor, small brass spark at the seam. Fast, economical, light-tier. No modern sport, no baseball, no anatomy chart, no labels. Empty space in the top quarter. No text.

## Bear Hug Break — Warrior, design cost 9 (not canon)

Stylized ability card, heavy impact. Tight crop on the break point. A stocky Warrior's elbow drives outward and peels an enemy's fingers off the forearm; the grip is failing, knuckles slipping, fingers splayed, a visible gap where the hold just opened. Asymmetric silhouette: Warrior expanding on a diagonal, enemy shoved back. Explosive, costly, not a struggle-in-progress and not two people locked in a hug. No bear, no animal. Empty space in the top quarter. No text.
