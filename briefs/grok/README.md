# Grok — card design and art

Standing lane: card faces and game art, with full design authority on faces.

Entry points: `briefs/grok/` (work queue). Art reference: `art/reference/`.
Output: finished faces to `art/card-faces/<class>/` on branch `grok/<slug>`,
with DONE.md.

Standing rules:

- The effect must read from the face alone. Action-focused, simple,
  archetype-clear.
- Stronger abilities must visibly read as more expensive (windup or recovery
  frames, heavier composition).
- Style: stylized and easy on the eyes — not realistic, gritty, or weathered.
- Brutus: a young man in his twenties, softer face, pugilist-style fist
  fighter. Grounded in `art/reference/brutus-pugilist-reference.jpg`.
- Current redo priority (from the 2026-09-12 audit): Called Shot, Blinding
  Halo, Stigmata, Bear Hug Break first, then the remaining firm redos.
