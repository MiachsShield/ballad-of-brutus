# Brief: dungeon character rendering style target (2026-09-16)

From Robert, via Kavi. This is new binding art direction — it extends the
visual guide, it does not replace the locked Brutus spec.

## The target

`art/visual-guide/dungeon-character-style-reference.jpg` (also mirrored at
`workspace/brutus-art/reference/dungeon-character-style-reference.jpg`
locally) shows how characters should look **inside the dungeon**: Brutus,
companions, enemies.

- Stylized anime 3D in the Wizardry Variants Daphne register: clean readable
  sculpts, expressive faces, cloth and gear that describe the role at a
  glance.
- Characters must read at corridor scale — silhouette and role clear from
  the player's camera distance, against stone-dungeon geometry.
- Action-comedy register throughout: Bravely charm and punch timing, not
  Souls grit, not carnival sparks.

## What it is NOT

- The figure in the reference is a short female. She is NOT Brutus. Take the
  style, the proportions-in-space, and the readability — not the character.
- The locked Brutus spec still governs Brutus himself: early-to-mid 20s,
  shaved head / close buzz, stocky and brawny, strong jaw, young and not
  weathered. Render THAT man in THIS style.
- The measurement overlays (50cm markers, perspective boxes) are scale
  calibration from the source — useful for proportion, not part of the look.
