  live, scheme, fall in love, rob each other, walk out and die on their own, and it all shows up
  in the new **Bulletin**.
- Design and code notes: `docs/personality-experiment.md`.

## How to play it

1. Play the game normally for 20+ turns: recruit, take expeditions, gift and visit people.
2. Read the **Bulletin** (sidebar) every turn or two. Click names; open profiles.
3. Back two or three people from their profiles (*Back them*). Mix ranks.
4. Take at least three expeditions with your party: one you finish, one you leave, one you lose if you can.
5. Keep going to 40+ turns.

## What to report

1. **One population?** Does anyone you met through the game (a recruit, a guest, a rival) also turn
   up in the bulletin doing their own thing? Does anything feel like two worlds stitched together?
2. **Does it feel like people?** Do names start to stick, and can you predict who will do what?
3. **Bulletin.** Right three stories? Do the bystander lines fit who is speaking?
4. **Dominoes.** Open *Trace this story* on anything surprising. Do the steps make sense?
5. **Your company.** Did a companion get caught up in the world (robbed, seduced, a lover, a feud)?
   Did a companion's death on an expedition become a story?
6. **Stakes.** Did backing someone feel like a bet? Did either end (gifts unasked, betrayal, snubs) happen?
7. **Expeditions.** Did the town react to how they went? Did someone claim an opening you left?
8. **Pace.** Is End Turn noticeably slower than the main build?
9. **Breakage.** Errors, empty bulletins, a person dead in one place and alive in another, numbers
   that run away (World numbers on the Bulletin screen). Screenshot plus the turn number.

Known: a few card faces fail to load in the dungeon (same in the main build).
